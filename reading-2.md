## What to Read

- **Batatia, Kovács, Simm, Ortner, Csányi**, *MACE: Higher Order Equivariant Message Passing Neural
  Networks for Fast and Accurate Force Fields.* NeurIPS 35, 11423-11436, 2022. [arXiv:2206.07697](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.07697)
- **Batatia et al.**, *A foundation model for atomistic materials chemistry* (MACE-MP-0). J. Chem.
  Phys. 163, 184110, 2025. [doi:10.1063/5.0297006](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article-lookup/doi/10.1063/5.0297006)
- **Creed et al.**, *Six Open Questions in Machine-Learned Interatomic Potential Foundation
  Models.*, 2026, [arXiv:2606.07327](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07327).

Peter will be using Sunday to pull together materials for discussions, so it's
fine to submit late on Saturday.

Advice on how to read:

- Budget about four hours for the set, and skip all supplementary material.
- The NeurIPS paper is short but dense. Try to understand it. Document what you
  don't understand in your Gradescope submission. The goal is to have you see a
  complete description of the MACE framework.
- The J. Chem. Phys. paper assumes of a lot of chemistry background in Section
  II -- get a high level understanding but if the detail is hard to understand
  then just skim that part and include some questions about what was confusing
  in what you upload to Gradescope.  Don't worry about all of the details in
  Section VI. The goal is to have you see an impressive application of MACE. 
- The Creed paper will be the focus of our discussion. Focus on really
  understanding a few of the open problems. The goal is to have you understand
  where the frontier of research is in MLIPs.

## What to submit
Submit a PDF on Gradescope that contains the following information.

**Q1 (Basic Info).** What is your name and netid? What paper(s) are you reporting on?

**Q2 (Paper Summary).** Summarize each paper you are reporting on. If the paper is a research
paper, state the paper's claimed research contributions. If the paper is a review or survey paper,
state what area they survey, and state some of the major ideas and goals of the area. If you are
reporting on multiple papers, provide a separate summary for each one.

**Q3 (Paper Comments).** Answer at least two of the questions below about the paper(s) in the
reading assignment. If you are reporting on multiple papers you don't need to give separate
answers for each paper.

- What did you find interesting or exciting about the paper(s)?
- What do you think the paper(s) could have improved?
- What ideas for future research did the paper(s) inspire?
- What did you find confusing about the paper(s)?

**Q4 (optional).** Are there any other thoughts you would like to share?

## Rules

Read the papers and write your answers **without an LLM**. LLMs are a good tool for getting a
quick understanding of many papers, but really understanding a paper requires you to actually read
it. Afterward, if you would like to ask an LLM to explain something you did not understand, you
may do this, and you may edit your submission if it changes what you want to say.

Reading assignments are **required in every registration option** --- 3 credits graded, 2 credits
graded, and 2 credits S/U. Students taking the course for 2 credits S/U are required to submit
for all but one of the paper discussion days.

## Making the PDF

- Any editor works. Export or print to PDF. A legible photo or scan of handwriting is also fine.
- Label your answers Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4. If Gradescope asks you to assign pages to questions, assign
  the page containing each answer.
