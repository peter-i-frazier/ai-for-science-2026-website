**Tue, Nov 10, 12:00--1:00pm**, 453 Rhodes Hall. Not a class meeting. Open to everyone. Lunch provided.

June Andrews and Scott Clark, both Cornell CAM PhD alumni, will hold a panel discussion. Scott also gives a [guest lecture](scott-clark.html) in class on Mon, Nov 9.

## Applying Math in the Household: The AI Architecture Behind The Home CEO

**June Andrews**, building The Home CEO

### Abstract

Households are dense with applied mathematics problems: growth curves, commute optimization, immunization schedules, childcare planning, financial projections, just to start. To help a household tackle the breadth of challenges, without losing sight of household goals and tradeoffs at the household level, The Home CEO operates on a novel AI Architecture. We link organizational tools, household context, and personalized mathematical models as a platform on which AI can orchestrate solutions for Moms running their households. Here we examine the case study of The Home CEO, where it breaks from traditional ML systems and where it will continue to grow in the age of AI.

### Bios

June Andrews is building The Home CEO, an AI platform of personalized mathematical models for running a household. She previously led data science and machine learning work at LinkedIn, Pinterest, GE Digital, Stitch Fix, and Nike, where she was Senior Director of Search Sciences.

Scott Clark is co-founder and CEO of Distributional, building Talaria Scientific, a multi-agent harness for computational science research. He previously co-founded SigOpt, a Bayesian optimization company acquired by Intel in 2020, and then served as Intel's VP and GM of AI and HPC Supercomputing.
