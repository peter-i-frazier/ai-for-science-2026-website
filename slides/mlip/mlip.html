Speaker notes. Unit 07. Depends on unit 06 (GNN). STRUCTURE (40+1 slides): 2-3 Engineering motivation. Two atoms, one coordinate, one energy curve -- then what knowing that curve lets you predict. 5-6 The potential energy surface, generalised from 2 atoms to N. 7-10 WAY 1, quantum mechanics: what a wavefunction is (with a real computed H2 density), Schrodinger + energy-as-a-minimisation, DFT as a change of decision variable, and what DFT costs. 11-12 WAY 2, classical force fields, and the gap between the two. 13+ What an MLIP is, the architectures, the reading. Appendix: universal potentials, the leaderboard, the two critiques. THE ONE SENTENCE THE UNIT TURNS ON is on "Fit the surface instead of deriving it": an MLIP replaces the INNER minimisation -- the step where, for one fixed set of nuclei, you minimise over the electrons to get an energy. Not the dynamics. Say it slowly. The class has no science background. Slides 7-9 are where to go slowly. Slide 7's left panel is a genuine minimal-basis LCAO calculation that integrates to exactly 2 electrons, and the right panel is the SAME curve from slide 2 with the current bond length marked -- point at both and say "one nuclear configuration, one point."
